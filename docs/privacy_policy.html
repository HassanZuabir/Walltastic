Privacy Policy — Walltastic

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Walltastic ("the app", "we") is a wallpaper gallery app published by HZ Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we collect

Walltastic does not require an account and does not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information on our own servers. We operate no backend of our own.

  • Saved favorites and settings (theme, Wi-Fi-only downloads) are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • Downloaded wallpapers are saved to your device's photo gallery at your request.

Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may process data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Pexels API — wallpapers are fetched from Pexels. Your IP address is visible to Pexels when images are loaded. See the Pexels Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — the app shows ads served by Google. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch wallpapers and serve ads.
  • Photos / media access — only used to save wallpapers you choose to download.

Children

Walltastic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured through Google AdMob, which complies with applicable children's privacy regulations.

Data retention and deletion

All app data (favorites, settings) lives on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes it completely. Downloaded wallpapers remain in your gallery until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above and, where appropriate, noted in the app's release notes.
